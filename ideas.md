# CS130 Semester Project Ideas

## Dataset 1: Food
**Link:** https://corgis-edu.github.io/corgis/csv/food/

**Questions:**
- Which types of food contain the most calories?
  
-  \* Is there a relationship between the amount of sugar and calories in different foods?

**How I would answer this question:**
- I would use the sugar and calorie values for each food in the dataset
- I would compare the calorie amounts of foods with higher sugar content to those with lower sugar content
- I would create a scatter plot with sugar on the x-axis and calories on the y-axis to see if there is a relationship
- I would look for a pattern to determine whether foods with more sugar generally have more calories
