# CS130 Semester Project Ideas

## Dataset 1: Food
**Link:** https://corgis-edu.github.io/corgis/csv/food/

**Questions:**
- Which types of food contain the most sugar?
  
- \* Is there a relationship between protein and fat content in different foods?

**How I would answer this question:**
- I would use the `Data.Protein` and `Data.Fat.Total Lipid` columns from the Food dataset to compare the protein and fat content of different foods
- I would check for any missing values in these columns so they do not affect my analysis
- I would create a scatter plot with protein on the x-axis and fat on the y-axis to visualize the relationship between the two nutrients
- I would look for patterns in the scatter plot to determine whether foods with higher protein content generally contain more or less fat
